# Hybrid AR-diffusion models, in-model draft/verify, MTP/mask-token heads, and speculative decoding on reasoning traces (2025-2026)

Scope note: built from ~25 search/fetch calls on 2026-11-02. Most numbers come from fetched arXiv HTML/abstract pages (fetched via a summarizing tool, so exact table values should be spot-checked against the PDFs before citing in a paper). Items marked [abstract only] mean only the abstract page was read. Items marked [not fetched] come from search-result titles or prior knowledge and must be verified.

## Q1. Which models do drafting and verification in one network, how do they get target-quality computation over drafted tokens, and what are batch-1 speedups?

### Takeaway
Two concrete 2025-2026 designs do draft+verify inside one network with near-AR quality: NVIDIA's TiDAR (one forward pass: causal verification of the previous block plus bidirectional one-step diffusion drafting of the next block, 4.71x / 5.91x tokens/s at batch 1 for 1.5B / 8B) and Nemotron-Labs-Diffusion (tri-mode AR / diffusion / self-speculation, ~6x tokens per forward for 8B instruct, up to 3.3-3.97x real throughput on GB200). Both buy this with large continued pretraining (50B-150B tokens for TiDAR; 1T AR + 300B joint tokens for Nemotron-Labs-Diffusion). Target-quality computation over drafted tokens comes from running the verification pass through the full causal network, either in the same forward (TiDAR, quadratic SS) or in a second causal pass reusing the prefix KV cache (linear SS).

### Cited Findings
**TiDAR: Think in Diffusion, Talk in Autoregression (arXiv 2511.08923, Nov 2025; MLSys 2026 oral)**
- Mechanism: each step's input = prefix (cached KV) + tokens drafted in the previous step + mask slots for the next step. Previous drafts are verified by AR rejection sampling under causal attention; next-step drafts are predicted in parallel with bidirectional attention within the draft block, "conditioned on all possible acceptance outcomes". Prefix causal, only decoding block bidirectional, so KV caching is exact and rejected-token KV is evicted. Drafting is one-step diffusion. — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.08923v1)
- Training: appends masks equal to sequence length (doubles training sequence length); AR loss on shifted labels plus diffusion loss on all masked positions, balance alpha usually 1. TiDAR 1.5B: continued pretraining from Qwen2.5-1.5B on 50B tokens (block 4/8/16). TiDAR 8B: from Qwen3-8B on 150B tokens (block 16). H100s, 2M-token global batch, seq len 4096, LR 1e-5 to 3e-6 cosine. No GPU-hour figure reported. — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.08923v1)
- Speed (batch 1, single H100, native PyTorch + Flex Attention): 1.5B 4.71x tokens/s over Qwen2.5-1.5B at 7.45 tokens/NFE; 8B 5.91x over Qwen3-8B at 8.25 tokens/NFE ("trust diffusion" mode). Reported to beat EAGLE-3 open weights on speedup. — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.08923v1)
- Quality: generative (code+math) avg 1.5B 44.03 vs Qwen2.5-1.5B 41.64 vs Block Diffusion 1.5B 38.41; 8B 65.31 (trust diffusion) / 63.90 (trust AR) vs Qwen3-8B 68.09, Dream-7B 58.74, LLaDA-8B 41.78. Likelihood tasks: 1.5B 64.43 vs 65.16; 8B 75.40 vs 74.25. Claimed lossless at 1.5B relative to its AR counterpart; "minimal loss" at 8B, i.e. NOT lossless vs the original Qwen3-8B (because the backbone itself was retrained). — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.08923v1)
- Stated limitations/future work: only batch 1 benchmarked; doubled training sequence length makes long-context training costly (context parallelism for TiDAR deferred); custom kernels/scheduling not done; remaining gap to fine-tuned AR models may close with more data. — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.08923v1)

**Nemotron-Labs-Diffusion: A Tri-Mode Language Model Unifying AR, Diffusion, and Self-Speculation Decoding (arXiv 2607.05722, Jul 2026; Fu, Whalen, Kautz, Molchanov et al., NVIDIA by naming/hardware, affiliation not stated on page)**
- Mechanism: joint loss L_AR + 0.3 L_diff with a dual-stream input (noised view + clean causal view) so both losses come from one pass without label leakage. Self-speculation: append k masks, denoise in one diffusion pass, then verify with a causal pass reusing prefix KV; accept longest prefix matching AR predictions plus one AR token (two forwards per cycle). Optional rank-128 LoRA on o_proj (~0.4% params) as drafter, trained with distribution matching + CE against the AR verifier. Quadratic variant does draft+verify in one forward with a structured mask. — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.05722v1)
- Training: from Ministral3 3B/8B/14B; 1T tokens pure AR, then 300B tokens joint AR-diffusion (Nemotron Nano 2 data), 256 H100s; instruct SFT on 45B tokens. — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.05722v1)
- Speed at batch 1, tokens per forward (8B instruct): diffusion 2.57x, LoRA linear SS 5.99x, quadratic SS 6.38x; 8B base: 2.06x / 4.67x / 7.04x; linear SS LoRA 3B 4.36x, 14B 5.96x. Real throughput: GB200 up to 3.3x (3.97x with optimized kernel, 1015 tok/s); RTX Pro 6000 277/525 tok/s FP8/INT4; DGX Spark 77.5/112.5 tok/s; vs EAGLE-3 2.4x (GB200), 2.3x, 1.8x. Average acceptance length at draft length 31 (SPEED-Bench): native 5.46, LoRA 6.82, EAGLE-3 2.75, MTP 4.24. — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.05722v1)
- Quality: 8B instruct avg AR 63.61, diffusion 63.18, LoRA linear SS 62.81; diffusion training lowered IFEval 71.66 -> 68.65 and HumanEval 82.93 -> 80.49. Not claimed strictly lossless; small SS vs AR gap attributed to kernel mismatch between single-token decode and multi-token prefill. All evals in non-thinking mode. — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.05722v1)
- Stated limitations: confidence sampler reaches ~3x TPF vs 7.60x speed-of-light ceiling; prefix-only verification discards confident non-prefix tokens; accuracy best at block 8-16 and degrades for larger blocks; quadratic SS slower on real hardware (FlexAttention); tested only to 14B; "weak long-range parallelism", generation still leans left-to-right; future: better draft-verifier alignment, nested subnets for cheaper drafting, better samplers, segment/paragraph-level parallelism. — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.05722v1)

**SDAR: A Synergistic Diffusion-AutoRegression Paradigm (arXiv 2510.06303, Oct 2025; ACL 2026 Findings) [abstract only]**
- Converts a trained AR model into a blockwise diffusion model with "brief, data-efficient" adaptation; AR across blocks, parallel discrete diffusion within block. Claims AR-level performance; 30B MoE model surpasses its AR counterpart on GPQA and ChemBench; larger models more robust to block size/threshold. No numbers on the abstract page. Not a draft-verify scheme (no exact verification). — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.06303v3)
- Nemotron-Labs-Diffusion reports its diffusion mode is +9.09% over SDAR-8B Chat (instruct). — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.05722v1)

**Jacobi Forcing (arXiv 2512.14681, Dec 2025; UCSD/Snowflake/SJTU)** — one model, causal parallel decoding
- Progressive distillation on the model's own Jacobi trajectories, noise-aware causal attention (causal mask unchanged), progressive consistency loss + weighted AR loss; rounds with block 16 then 32, 10k steps each, LR 1e-6, batch 4. Qwen2.5-Coder-7B-Instruct trained on ~450k OpenCodeInstruct prompts. — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.14681v1)
- HumanEval: AR 1.00 TPF / 41.3 TPS / 87.8 acc; Jacobi Forcing 4.01 TPF / 159.5 TPS (3.86x) / 83.5; with multi-block + rejection recycling 4.09 TPF / 3.97x / 83.5. MBPP 2.74 TPF, 2.57x, acc 70.4 vs 74.3. Model not lossless (accuracy drop); decoding add-ons lossless w.r.t. the distilled model. Speedup saturates with more training steps (attributed to distribution shift). Batch size of timing not stated in excerpt. — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.14681v1)

**Block diffusion / Esoteric LMs [not fetched]**
- BD3-LM (Arriola et al., "Block Diffusion", arXiv 2503.09573, ICLR 2025) and Esoteric LMs (Sahoo et al., arXiv 2506.01928) are the base architectures TiDAR/SDAR/DFlash build on; I did not fetch them in this pass, so no numbers here. TiDAR's "Block Diffusion 1.5B" baseline is 38.41 generative avg. — [TiDAR arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.08923v1)

### Inferences
- The "free" verification in TiDAR/Nemotron comes from retraining the whole backbone on a joint objective, which is why both lose a little vs the original AR checkpoint (TiDAR 8B 65.31 vs 68.09 generative; Nemotron 62.81 vs 63.61). Neither preserves an existing frozen target exactly; the gated-LoRA (Apple) line is the only one-network design here that keeps the next-token path bit-identical.
- Batch-1 tokens-per-forward around 6-8 for 8B in-model designs is comparable to DFlash's tau ~6.5 on Qwen3-8B (non-thinking), so the in-model approach is not clearly ahead in acceptance, only in avoiding a separate drafter.

### Gaps
- No GPU-hour training cost reported for TiDAR or Nemotron-Labs-Diffusion (only token counts and GPU type).
- No thinking-mode/long-CoT evaluation for TiDAR or Nemotron-Labs-Diffusion (Nemotron explicitly non-thinking only).
- SDAR exact numbers, BD3-LM and Eso-LM numbers not read in this pass.
- Did not find Google or Inception Labs technical reports with comparable in-model draft/verify numbers; ICLR 2027 OpenReview submissions not searched.

## Q2. Per-offset accuracy of mask-token / lookahead / MTP heads on a frozen or lightly adapted AR target, and data scaling

### Takeaway
Exact per-offset top-1 accuracy at offsets 4 and 8 for mask-token heads on a frozen target was not found in any fetched paper. Papers report either average accepted length (Apple: 1.67 / 2.69 / 3.17 tokens per step for 1 / 4 / 8 masks on Tulu3-8B after ~1M examples x 50k iterations) or chained per-step acceptance (FastMTP on frozen MiMo-7B: ~80% / 56% / 36% at draft steps 1/2/3 after 389K self-distilled samples). The user's slot-gated LoRA numbers (0.68 / 0.31 at offsets 1/4 after 8000 sequences) were trained on roughly two orders of magnitude less data than these references.

### Cited Findings
**Your LLM Knows the Future (Apple, arXiv 2507.11851, Jul 2025)**
- Mechanism: k=8 mask tokens appended; gated LoRA rank 128 active only at mask positions so next-token outputs are unchanged; 2-layer MLP sampler head conditioning on previous sampled token embedding + mask hidden state; consistency loss pulling each mask hidden state toward the (detached) hidden state of the corresponding true next-token position; linear decoding or quadratic decoding (k^2 masks interleaved, tree-style bias). — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.11851v1)
- Training: Tulu3-8B SFT base, Tulu3 data (~1M examples), 50,000 iterations on 8 A100s, batch 1 per GPU, LR 2e-4 after 5k warmup; only LoRA + sampler trained. — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.11851v1)
- Average accepted tokens per step: 1 mask 1.67, 4 masks 2.69, 8 masks 3.17. Per-domain 1 -> 8 masks: HumanEval 1.86 -> 5.35, GSM8K 1.84 -> 5.22, AlpacaEval 1.61 -> 2.52, IFEval 1.55 -> 2.39, MMLU 1.54 -> 2.38, PopQA 1.48 -> 1.91, TruthfulQA 1.55 -> 2.19. Per-offset accuracy at offsets 1/4/8 is NOT reported. Before fine-tuning, correct future token is within top-200 logits; after, within top-10. — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.11851v1)
- Lossless claim: "without any loss in quality" (gated LoRA keeps NTP path identical; standard verification). — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.11851v1)
- Data/rank scaling: ranks 1-16 already give reasonable speedup without sampler head; ranks above 128 reduce speedup (possibly overfitting on small SFT set). Standard (ungated) LoRA degrades ARC-Challenge and next-token loss; gated keeps them flat. — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.11851v1)
- Limitations: gated LoRA cannot be merged into weights (small overhead); speedup measured by acceptance, not wall-clock; diminishing returns with more masks; gains domain-dependent (code/math best); only SFT tested; pretraining-stage MTP and diffusion-based multi-token generation as future work. — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.11851v1)

**FastMTP (arXiv 2509.18362, Sep 2025)**
- Single shared-weight MTP head reused recursively (EAGLE-style), main model frozen, 210.8M trainable params (<3% of 7.83B MiMo-7B-RL), 389.4K self-distilled samples (EN+ZH, general/math/code), 3 epochs, <1 day on one H20 server. — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.18362v1)
- Per-step acceptance (averaged across tasks): k=1 ~80% (81% in intro), k=2 ~56%, k=3 ~36%; vanilla MTP ~70% / ~10% / ~2%. 2.03x avg speedup at K=3 on A10, batch 1, greedy, 1024-token cap; vanilla MTP 1.21x. Lossless (full-vocab verification). — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.18362v1)

**P-EAGLE (AWS, arXiv 2602.01469, Feb 2026)**
- Parallel drafting in an EAGLE drafter: position 1 uses real target hidden states; positions 2..K use a learnable shared hidden state plus learnable mask-token embedding; all K tokens in one forward. Hidden states from layers 2, L/2, L-1. — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.01469v1)
- Acceptance length at K=5 (avg HumanEval/MT-Bench/GSM8K): GPT-OSS-120B 3.1 (EAGLE-3) -> 3.3; GPT-OSS-20B 3.7 -> 3.7; Qwen3-Coder-30B 3.5 -> 3.6; vLLM end-to-end 1.10-1.36x over AR EAGLE-3. Per-position rates not reported. — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.01469v1)
- Scaling: training context length 1K/4K/8K/20K gives acceptance 2.4/2.8/2.9/3.0 (GPT-OSS-120B, MT-Bench); 20 -> 60 epochs +2.0% HumanEval, +4.6% MT-Bench. — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.01469v1)

**DFlash (arXiv 2602.06036, Feb 2026; ICML 2026)** — separate drafter, reference for the user's comparison
- 5 draft layers (8 for Qwen3-Coder), block 16; hidden states from 5 target layers fused and injected into K/V of every draft layer ("KV injection"); embeddings and LM head shared and frozen; random anchors with exponentially decaying position weights. ~800K samples (Nemotron Post-Training V2 + CodeAlpaca) with target-generated responses; ablations at 100K. — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.06036v2)
- Non-thinking, T=0: Qwen3-8B avg 4.86x, tau 6.49 (EAGLE-3: 1.76x/2.96 at 16, 2.07x/3.40 at 60); Qwen3-4B 4.91x, tau 6.54. T=1: Qwen3-8B 4.03x, tau 5.48. Per-position acceptance not reported in the fetched content. — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.06036v2)

**Nemotron-Labs-Diffusion self-spec acceptance by block (in-model reference)**
- SOL acceptance ratio from 2.89x (B=4) to 7.60x (B=32); linear SS 6.82x at B=32 (trained jointly, not frozen target). — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.05722v1)

### Inferences
- FastMTP's step-k acceptance is conditional chain acceptance with an autoregressive head (each step sees the previous drafted token), so it is not directly comparable to independent mask-slot top-1 at offset k; still, it shows a frozen-target head reaching ~36% at step 3 with ~390K samples.
- Apple's 4-mask average of 2.69 accepted tokens per step implies per-offset accept rates well above the user's 0.31 at offset 4 (rough back-of-envelope, not a paper number); Apple used ~1M examples x 50k iterations, plus a sampler head and consistency loss. The user's 8000-sequence run is very likely in the data-limited regime; P-EAGLE and DFlash both show acceptance still rising with data/context/epochs.
- DFlash's per-offset advantage likely comes partly from KV injection of multi-layer target features into every draft layer, which a LoRA on mask slots in the target only partially replicates (the slots do see all target layers, but share weights with the NTP path except for LoRA).

### Gaps
- No fetched paper reports per-offset top-1 accuracy at offsets 1/4/8 for mask-token heads; no clean data-scaling curve (accuracy vs number of training sequences) for mask-token/gated-LoRA heads.
- Did not fetch PARD, Medusa-2-era follow-ups, or "On multi-token prediction for efficient LLM inference" (arXiv 2502.09419, appeared in search) which may analyze frozen-backbone MTP limits.

## Q3. Why block/diffusion drafters lose acceptance on reasoning/thinking traces, and proposed fixes

### Takeaway
DFlash reports lower tau in thinking mode (Qwen3-8B T=0: GPQA 5.17, MATH-500 5.82, AIME25 5.74, vs 6.49 avg non-thinking) without explaining it. No block/diffusion-drafter paper found gives a mechanistic explanation. The closest evidence is an AR-drafter diagnostic on R1-Distill traces: acceptance does not decay with position, but rejections concentrate on reflection triggers ("wait", "however", "check"), i.e. an entropy-at-decision-point failure; a LoRA alignment fix targeting those tokens did not help. Block-drafter improvements in 2026 (D2SD, D-Loop, ReTrace, DDTree, DSpark) target suffix decay / single-trajectory verification generally, and none reports thinking-mode results.

### Cited Findings
- DFlash thinking mode (Table 2, drafters trained on reasoning traces), T=0: Qwen3-4B GPQA 4.23x (tau 5.23), MATH-500 4.59x (5.74), AIME25 4.39x (5.54); Qwen3-8B GPQA 4.17x (5.17), MATH-500 4.64x (5.82), AIME25 4.51x (5.74). Paper gives no explanation for the gap vs non-thinking (~6.5-7.8). Also: drafters degrade beyond 4K context; fine-tuning on longer data recovers much of it (Table 4). — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.06036v2)
- Speculative Acceptance Does Not Decay with Position (Zhiming Meng, arXiv 2610.30644, 22 Oct 2026, single-author, not peer reviewed): MATH-500 (500 problems), target DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B, drafts R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B and Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, greedy and sampling, ~1.78M verification rounds. After removing a greedy repetition pathology, acceptance does not decay with position (greedy 0-8K, sampling 0-12K); second half slightly higher than first. Rejections concentrate on reflection triggers ("wait", "however", "check"), "entropy-selective failure". Temperature sweep suggests sampling-mode drop on reflection tokens is an argmax-agreement visibility artifact. Three-arm LoRA trajectory alignment (uniform, reflection-marked, entropy-weighted) did not improve mean accepted length. Uses AR drafts, not block drafters. [abstract only] — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30644)
- D-Loop (arXiv 2610.06011, Oct 2026): identifies a "repetition trap" in block diffusion drafters (independent per-position marginals emit adjacent repeats; since only a contiguous prefix is accepted, an early repeat kills the rest of the block). Two-pass drafting with the same drafter, second pass conditions on a confidence-selected prefix and regenerates the suffix. Adjacent-repeat rate pass 1 -> pass 2: GSM8K 19.4% -> 5.1%, LiveCodeBench 28.8% -> 4.9%, MT-Bench 44.5% -> 9.0%. Claims gains over DFlash and DSpark on Qwen3-4B/8B across 8 benchmarks (numbers not in fetched excerpt); second pass roughly doubles drafting time. No thinking-mode results in excerpt. — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.06011v1)
- D2SD: Dual Diffusion Draft Models (arXiv 2606.04446, 2026; PKU/Tsinghua/HKUST/UIUC/Ant): DFlash-style drafter + variable-prefix drafter that re-anchors at top-K likely rejection points from DFlash confidence; K+1 shared-prefix candidates verified jointly with cascade attention. Qwen3-8B T=0 avg: DFlash 4.16x / alpha 5.31, D2SD 4.98x / 7.05, EAGLE-3 3.80x / 5.91; T=1: 3.43x/4.33, 4.01x/5.45, 3.34x/5.33; GPT-OSS-20B T=0: 3.53x/4.13, 6.15x/8.02, 2.35x/2.85. DFlash confidence roughly calibrated to empirical accept rates. No thinking-mode experiments; gains smaller on chat; third cascade level raised alpha 7.62 -> 8.24 but lowered speedup 5.35x -> 5.13x. (Note DFlash numbers here differ from DFlash's own paper, different data/eval.) — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.04446)
- DSpark (DeepSeek, arXiv 2607.05147, Jul 2026) [abstract only]: parallel backbone + lightweight sequential module to reduce suffix decay; confidence-scheduled verification length per request; in DeepSeek-V4 production serving, +60-85% per-user generation speed over MTP-1 baseline at matched throughput. No thinking-mode or exact acceptance numbers on the page. — [HF papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.05147)
- ReTrace (arXiv 2608.29748, Aug 2026) [abstract only]: conditions the next DFlash draft block on hidden states of the rejected suffix from the previous round (gated residual fusion, target-aware correction), lossless, no extra forward; "consistently improves" acceptance and speed over DFlash on Qwen3 math/code/dialogue; no numbers, no thinking mode. — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29748v1)
- DDTree: Block Diffusion Draft Trees (Ringel and Romano, arXiv 2604.12989, Apr 2026) [abstract only]: best-first heap builds a draft tree from DFlash per-position distributions under a node budget, verified in one target pass with ancestor-only mask; motivation that DFlash verifies only one trajectory. No numbers in abstract. — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12989)
- Acceptance Dynamics Across Cognitive Domains (arXiv 2604.14682, single author, small T4 study, Llama-2-7B-GPTQ target, TinyLlama draft, 64-token cap): mean acceptance chat 0.565, code 0.538, reasoning (GSM8K) 0.532, math 0.518; entropy-acceptance correlation weak (rho -0.149 to -0.202). Too small and too short-context to say anything about thinking traces. — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.14682)
- Reasoning-specific speculation outside block drafters: Lookahead Reasoning (Fu et al., arXiv 2506.19830, Jun 2025, NeurIPS 2025): step-level speculation where draft proposes future reasoning steps, target expands them in a batch, a verifier keeps semantically correct steps; SD speedup improves from 1.4x to 2.1x on GSM8K/AIME etc. Not token-lossless (semantic verification). — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.19830); Self-Speculation for Faster Reasoning Models (SSR; UCLA, arXiv 2608.20359): training-free, uses a shorter-CoT-budget run of the same model to draft the answer; up to 24.1% latency reduction (Gemma-4-E4B ClassEval), only 2.9% on Qwen3.5-4B HumanEval because only the answer phase is accelerated. — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.20359v1)

### Inferences
- Combining Meng (rejections at reflection triggers, entropy-selective) with D-Loop/D2SD (block drafters lose the rest of the block after the first miss) suggests a plausible mechanism for DFlash's thinking-mode tau drop: thinking traces contain many high-entropy branch points ("wait", restarts, self-checks) per 16-token block, and block drafters with independent per-position marginals cannot recover after a branch-point miss. This is my inference; no paper tests it on block drafters.
- Context length is a second candidate: thinking traces are long, and DFlash reports degradation beyond 4K context. DFlash Table 2 does not state the token cap, so this cannot be separated from the trace-type effect from published numbers.
- None of the 2026 block-drafter fixes (D-Loop, D2SD, ReTrace, DDTree, DSpark) report thinking-mode results, so whether they close the ~5.5 vs ~6.5-7.9 gap is open.

### Gaps
- No paper found that measures per-position acceptance of a block/diffusion drafter on thinking traces, or that explains DFlash's thinking-mode gap.
- Meng's exact acceptance numbers not read (abstract only), and it uses AR drafters only.
- SpecDiff-2 (MLSys 2026 oral) and DART (arXiv 2601.19278) appeared in search but were not fetched.

## Q4. Recurring open problems authors report

### Takeaway
Across papers the recurring stated problems are: (1) prefix-only verification wastes confident later tokens and causes suffix decay; (2) quality gap vs the original AR model when the backbone is retrained; (3) training/long-context cost (doubled sequences, degradation beyond 4K); (4) gains concentrated in code/math, smaller in chat and under sampling; (5) only batch-1 or small-concurrency evaluation; (6) kernel/systems gaps between theoretical tokens-per-forward and wall clock.

### Cited Findings
- Prefix-only verification / non-contiguous acceptance: Nemotron-Labs-Diffusion suggests diffusion-based verification of non-contiguous drafts — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.05722v1); DDTree motivated by single-trajectory verification — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12989); DSpark "suffix decay" — [HF papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.05147); D-Loop repetition trap — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.06011v1)
- Sampler gap: Nemotron confidence sampler ~3x TPF vs 7.60x SOL — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.05722v1)
- Quality gap after backbone retraining: TiDAR 8B 65.31 vs Qwen3-8B 68.09; may close with more data — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.08923v1); Jacobi Forcing HumanEval 87.8 -> 83.5 — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.14681v1)
- Long context: TiDAR doubled sequence length — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.08923v1); DFlash degradation beyond 4K — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.06036v2); P-EAGLE acceptance rises with training context length — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.01469v1)
- Domain dependence and sampling: Apple (code/math ~5x vs chat ~2.5x) — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.11851v1); D2SD gains smaller on chat and at T=1 — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.04446)
- Batch size: TiDAR batch 1 only — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.08923v1); DFlash SGLang Qwen3-4B Math500 4.8x at concurrency 1 falling to 2.9x at 32 — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.06036v2)
- Weak long-range parallelism, block size accuracy tradeoff (best ~8-16) — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.05722v1)
- Non-mergeable gated LoRA overhead; acceptance-based rather than wall-clock speedup — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.11851v1)

### Inferences
- Thinking-mode evaluation is itself a recurring omission: Nemotron (explicitly non-thinking), TiDAR, D2SD, D-Loop excerpt, ReTrace, DSpark abstract all lack it; DFlash is the main block drafter reporting thinking-mode tau.

### Gaps
- Did not survey ICLR 2027 OpenReview submissions or Google/Inception Labs reports; there may be newer in-model draft/verify or reasoning-specific drafter work not captured here.
